THE UNICORN LULLABY — the universe's outro song and first Spotify single.
Inspiration: Humlan Djojj, "Djurens vaggvisa" — predictable tuck-in verses, real and unproduced feel.

SOUND: MAJOR KEY ONLY — bright, warm, safe; never minor/melancholy (Lars 2026-08-29). ~68 bpm, gentle HARP arpeggios (the magical thread, Lars 2026-08-29) + soft felt piano + warm nylon guitar, distant string pad, quiet close female voice
(a mother at a bedside, not a studio), no percussion, no build; final refrain nearly a hum;
gentle loop-friendly ending.

STRUCTURE: 3 verses (Pip sleeps, Moss sleeps, Princess sleeps beside Glitter) + refrain after each,
slower each time.

LYRICS:
V1: The sun has gone to sleep now, behind the rainbow trees / The flowers close their petals,
there's a slow and sleepy breeze / Little Pip has tucked his wings in, high in his mossy tree /
Goodnight, goodnight, little bird — sleep tight.
V2: The stream is running softly, it's singing to the stones / The fireflies are dimming, they
are drifting to their homes / Little Moss has curled his tail up, warm inside his cave /
Goodnight, goodnight, little dragon — sleep tight.
V3: The moon is on the water, at Moonwater Spring it glows / And Glitter's horn shines softly,
as the gentle darkness grows / And Princess, little Princess, lays her head on mama's side /
Goodnight, goodnight, little unicorn — sleep tight.
REFRAIN: Sleep now… sleep now… the forest holds you near / Sleep now… sleep now… your dreams
are safe in here.

DEPLOYMENT: soft/instrumental version = every episode's outro music; full song to streaming;
seeds the "Princess the Unicorn Lullabies" album (franchise vertical #4).
PRODUCTION PATH: ElevenLabs Music (needs key permission) for sung demos; consider REAL production
via the house music pipeline (felt piano = the catalog's own craft) for the release version.

OUTRO VERSION (Lars, 2026-08-29): every episode's outro SINGS the short lullaby —
the refrain + the goodnight tag (~25s): "Sleep now… sleep now… the forest holds you
near / Sleep now… sleep now… your dreams are safe in here / Goodnight, goodnight,
little unicorn — sleep tight." Same lines every episode, so children sing along.
