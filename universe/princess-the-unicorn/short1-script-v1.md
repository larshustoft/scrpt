# PRINCESS AND THE LOST BELL
## Short film 1 — SHOOTING MANUSCRIPT v1 (three and a half minutes)

A stand-alone short in the Princess the Unicorn universe: same cast, same
world, one small story. Built on the extended foundation (Lars, 2026-09-03):
pose sheets for every character, wide atlas plates for every place, one
approved image per object STATE. House rules: no clip longer than five
seconds, no lip sync — voices act over closed mouths.

**Voices** — STORYTELLER David *(warm, gentle, caring — never dramatic)*
· PRINCESS Tilly · GLITTER Beth · PIP Aurelia · MOSS Valf.

**Object set** — `glitter-bell` (states: on-the-ribbon / lying-in-grass /
back-on-the-ribbon) · `hollow-log` (states: empty / moss-peeking-out).

**Places (atlas)** — valley-from-above · meadow-by-the-river ·
hollow-log-clearing · path-home-dusk-wide.

---

## 1 · THE VALLEY

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, gentle)*: Far away, where the rainbows go to
rest, there is a valley.

**STORYTELLER** *(warm)*: In the valley lives a small unicorn called
Princess, and her mother, Glitter.

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, smiling)*: Around Glitter's neck hangs a small
silver bell. When Princess hears it, she knows her mother is near.

*The sound of the silver bell. Ring, ring.*

---

## 2 · THE QUIET MEADOW

**STORYTELLER** *(soft)*: One morning the meadow was very quiet.

**PRINCESS** *(small, calling)*: Mama?

**STORYTELLER** *(soft)*: No bell. Only the river, and the wind in the
grass.

**STORYTELLER** *(warm)*: Princess looked in the tall grass. She looked
by the river. She looked under the big leaves.

**PRINCESS** *(worried)*: I can't hear the bell.

---

## 3 · PIP

**STORYTELLER** *(brightening)*: Then a small blue bird landed on a stone
in front of her. It was Pip.

**PIP** *(cheerful, kind)*: Why the long face, Princess?

**PRINCESS** *(sad)*: Mama's bell is gone. Without the bell, I can't find
her.

**PIP** *(cheerful, certain)*: Then we will look with four eyes instead
of two.

**STORYTELLER** *(warm)*: Pip flew high and looked down. Princess walked
low and looked under things.

---

## 4 · THE HOLLOW LOG

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, wondering)*: By the old hollow log, something
shone in the grass.

**PIP** *(excited)*: There! Something silver!

**STORYTELLER** *(warm)*: It was the bell. Its ribbon had come loose, and
it had rolled into the grass.

**STORYTELLER** *(gentle)*: But the bell had rolled far under the log,
and Princess was too big to crawl in.

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, amused)*: Out from the hollow log came a little
green dragon, shaking from his nose to the end of his tail.

**MOSS** *(frightened, small)*: Is it a snake? I am afraid of snakes.

**PRINCESS** *(kind, gentle)*: It is not a snake, Moss. It is my mama's
bell.

**MOSS** *(relieved, brave)*: Oh. Then I can help. I am not afraid of
bells.

**STORYTELLER** *(warm)*: Moss reached in with his long tail and pushed
the bell along the grass, all the way to Princess.

---

## 5 · TING

**STORYTELLER** *(warm)*: Princess picked up the ribbon in her teeth. The
bell went ting.

*The bell. One clear ring.*

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, hopeful)*: And far across the meadow, someone
heard it.

**STORYTELLER** *(bright)*: Glitter came running.

**GLITTER** *(relieved, tender)*: Princess! I have been looking everywhere
for you.

**PRINCESS** *(happy, a little proud)*: And I have been looking everywhere
for you. Your bell fell off.

**STORYTELLER** *(warm)*: Glitter bent her head, and Princess hung the
bell back on its ribbon.

**GLITTER** *(warm, certain)*: Now we will never lose each other.

---

## 6 · HOME

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, gentle)*: That evening, three friends walked home
together along the golden path.

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, smiling)*: And every step of the way, the little
bell went ting, ting, ting.

*The bell, three soft rings, fading.*

**STORYTELLER** *(soft)*: The end.
