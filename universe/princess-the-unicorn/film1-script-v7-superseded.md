# PRINCESS AND THE HIDDEN SPRING
## Episode 1 — SHOOTING MANUSCRIPT v7 (trimmed to length)

Written to be followed with the eyes closed, by a child of four.
Trimmed from v6 to land the finished episode at 12–13 minutes. Nothing
was cut from the chain of causes. What went was the second version of a
joke, the third beat of banter, and every line where a character
repeated something the storyteller had already told us.

**The four laws of the writing** — name the thing, never point at it ·
name the people, never "they" · one idea per sentence · the storyteller
tells the story, the characters live inside it.

**The three laws of the story** — one rule, said early, naming the
meeting place · one event causes everything · the rule is what saves her.

**Voices** — STORYTELLER David *(warm, gentle, caring — never dramatic)*
· PRINCESS Tilly · GLITTER Beth · PIP Aurelia · MOSS Valf · FIREFLIES
Emily. Every line carries its own direction. Recording on eleven_v3.

---

## 1 · A MORNING IN RAINBOW FOREST

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, gentle)*: Far away, where the rainbows go to
rest, there is a forest. It is called Rainbow Forest. And every morning
in Rainbow Forest begins with the same happy sound.

*The sound of a silver bell. Ring, ring, ring.*

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, smiling)*: That is the sound of a little silver
bell. The bell belongs to a big, kind unicorn. Her name is Glitter. And
the little unicorn bouncing along behind her is her daughter. Her name
is Princess.

**PRINCESS** *(bright, friendly, to the audience)*: Good morning,
friend! My mum and I are going for our morning walk. Will you come?

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, gentle)*: Every morning, Princess and her mum
walk the same path to the same place. This morning began just like all
the others. But this morning would not end like the others.

---

## 2 · MAMA'S RULE

**STORYTELLER** *(warm)*: Princess and her mum walked in under the big
flowers. The morning mist lay soft on the path.

**GLITTER** *(warm, tender)*: Stay close to me, little one. Stay close
enough to hear my bell.

**PRINCESS** *(happy)*: I can always hear your bell, Mama. It sounds
like little stars laughing.

**GLITTER** *(gentle, teaching)*: And what if one day you cannot hear my
bell? Do you remember our rule?

**PRINCESS** *(proud, careful)*: I remember. Stop. Listen. And go to
Moonwater Spring.

**GLITTER** *(warm, certain)*: That is right. Stop walking. Listen for
my bell. If you cannot hear it, go to Moonwater Spring. That is our
meeting place.

**PRINCESS** *(small question)*: And you will come there too?

**GLITTER** *(tender, absolutely sure)*: Every time. I will always come
to Moonwater Spring to find you.

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, kind)*: Remember Mama's rule, friend. Stop.
Listen. Go to Moonwater Spring. Princess is going to need that rule
today.

---

## 3 · MOONWATER SPRING

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, wondering)*: The meeting place has a name. It is
called Moonwater Spring. Moonwater Spring is a little pool of water in a
green clearing. The water shines like the moon, even in the morning.

**STORYTELLER** *(gentle)*: And high above the pool stands a tall white
cliff, made of big white stones.

**PRINCESS** *(delighted)*: Mmm! This water tastes like starlight.

**GLITTER** *(warm, playful)*: And a little sprinkle of moonbeam, I
think.

**STORYTELLER** *(warm)*: The water from Moonwater Spring runs away
under the ground. It gives a drink to every flower and every tree in
Rainbow Forest.

**PRINCESS** *(curious)*: Does the whole forest drink this water, Mama?

**GLITTER** *(warm, simple)*: Every drop, my love.

**STORYTELLER** *(gentle, quietly marking it)*: Look up at the big white
stones, friend. Those stones have been sitting on that cliff for a
hundred years. In one minute, they will not be sitting there any more.

---

## 4 · THE STONES COME DOWN

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, calm)*: Princess and her mum walked back along
the path towards home. Then the forest made a sound.

*A long, low rumble.*

**PRINCESS** *(a little worried)*: Mama? What is that noise?

**GLITTER** *(calm, reassuring)*: It is only the old stones on the
cliff, my love. Stay close to me.

**STORYTELLER** *(gentle, clear)*: But it was more than that. High up on
the cliff, the big white stones came sliding down. Grey dust flew up
into the trees. The dust made a thick cloud all around Princess and her
mum.

**STORYTELLER** *(gentle, clear)*: And one round white stone rolled into
the little hole where the water comes out. The stone stuck fast. Now the
water could not get out at all. Nobody saw that stone, friend. Only you
and me.

**STORYTELLER** *(gentle)*: And down on the path, the shaking knocked a
branch out of a tree. The branch fell into the vines.

---

## 5 · THE LITTLE LIGHTS

**FIREFLIES** *(tiny, frightened)*: Help! Somebody help us, please!

**PRINCESS** *(alert, kind)*: Mama, wait! Somebody is calling for help!

**STORYTELLER** *(warm)*: Under the fallen branch was a family of
fireflies. Fireflies are tiny bugs with little lights inside them. The
branch had trapped them in the vines.

**PRINCESS** *(gentle, brave)*: Don't be frightened, little lights. I
will get you out.

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, careful)*: So Princess stayed by the branch.
Princess lifted the branch with her horn, very carefully. And every
single firefly wriggled out.

**FIREFLIES** *(bright, happy)*: We are free! Thank you, Princess!

**STORYTELLER** *(gentle, this is the turn)*: But while Princess was
helping the fireflies, her mum kept walking. Glitter was calling
Princess's name. Glitter was ringing her silver bell. But the grey dust
was thick between them, and the rumble was still rolling round the
trees. Princess did not hear her mum at all.

**PRINCESS** *(happy, then uncertain)*: I did it, Mama! Mama? Where are
you?

**STORYTELLER** *(gentle, sad but warm)*: And the path in front of
Princess was empty.

---

## 6 · STOP. LISTEN. GO.

**PRINCESS** *(calling, worried)*: Mama! Mama, where are you?

**STORYTELLER** *(gentle)*: Princess started to run down the path. Then
Princess stopped herself.

**PRINCESS** *(thinking it out loud)*: No. If I run, I might run the
wrong way.

**STORYTELLER** *(warm)*: Princess remembered the first part of Mama's
rule. Stop. Listen. So Princess stood very still, and Princess listened
as hard as she could.

**PRINCESS** *(hopeful, then small)*: Can you hear Mama's bell, friend?
No. I cannot hear it either.

**STORYTELLER** *(gentle, tender)*: There was no ringing anywhere. There
was no sound of Mama's bell in the whole forest. And for somebody very
small, that is a very big feeling.

**PRINCESS** *(frightened, small, trembling)*: I think I am lost.

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, encouraging)*: But Princess remembered the last
part of Mama's rule.

**PRINCESS** *(deciding, brave)*: Go to Moonwater Spring. Mama will come
to Moonwater Spring. That is our meeting place. I am going to walk
there.

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, clear)*: And now Princess had a plan, friend.
Princess was going to walk to Moonwater Spring. She was very small, and
she was not quite sure of the way. But she knew where she was going.

---

## 7 · PIP THE BIRD

**PIP** *(quick, cheerful, curious)*: Chirp! Hello! Who are you calling
for?

**STORYTELLER** *(warm)*: A little blue bird hopped out of the dust. The
bird was called Pip.

**PRINCESS** *(polite, hopeful)*: I am calling for my mum. She will be
at Moonwater Spring. But I don't know the way from here.

**PIP** *(bright, proud)*: Then it is lucky you met me! I know nearly
every path in Rainbow Forest. Moonwater Spring is that way. We go over
the rope bridge.

**PRINCESS** *(warm, relieved)*: Then let's walk there together.

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, kind)*: And that was the second good thing
Princess did today. When Princess could not do it on her own, Princess
asked for help.

---

## 8 · MOSS THE LITTLE DRAGON

**STORYTELLER** *(warm)*: Princess and Pip walked to the rope bridge.
The bridge hangs over a river, and it swings from side to side in the
wind.

**PRINCESS** *(wary)*: That bridge looks very wobbly.

**MOSS** *(nervous, shaky, small)*: Please don't step on my tail!

**STORYTELLER** *(warm)*: Out from under the bridge came a little green
dragon. He was shaking from his nose to the end of his tail. His name
was Moss.

**MOSS** *(nervous, small)*: I want to cross this bridge. But I am
afraid of high places. And I am afraid of deep water.

**PRINCESS** *(warm, inviting)*: Hello, Moss. My mum is waiting for me
at Moonwater Spring. Come with us.

**MOSS** *(worried)*: But I am not brave.

**PRINCESS** *(kind, certain)*: Being brave does not mean you are not
scared, Moss. Being brave means you keep going, even when you are.

**MOSS** *(small, gathering himself)*: Then maybe I can be brave for one
bridge.

**STORYTELLER** *(warm)*: And Princess and Pip and Moss crossed the rope
bridge together. Step by step. Slowly and carefully.

---

## 9 · THE SPRING HAS GONE DARK

**STORYTELLER** *(gentle, concerned)*: Princess and Pip and Moss came
into the green clearing. And Princess stopped very suddenly.

**PRINCESS** *(shocked, quiet)*: Look at the spring. The water has gone.

**STORYTELLER** *(gentle)*: Moonwater Spring was not shining any more.
There were only wet grey stones, and one thin little trickle of water.

**PRINCESS** *(working it out, then certain)*: Pip! Look up at the
cliff. The big white stones have fallen down. That was the rumble I
heard this morning. Now the water cannot get out.

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, proud of her)*: And Princess was right. Princess
and Pip and Moss followed the little trickle of water to a crack in the
cliff. Behind the crack there was a dark, quiet cave.

**MOSS** *(nervous, shaky)*: It is very dark in there.

**PRINCESS** *(worried, clear)*: But the water has to come out. The
whole forest drinks this water. And I am not going into a dark cave
without a light.

---

## 10 · THE LITTLE LIGHTS COME BACK

**PRINCESS** *(calling, hopeful)*: Little lights! Fireflies! Are you
there? We need your help!

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, delighted)*: And out from the trees came the
fireflies — the same family of fireflies that Princess had helped that
morning. The fireflies flew into the crack in the cliff, one after
another, and their little lights made a golden path into the dark.

**MOSS** *(small, honest)*: I am still afraid, Princess.

**PRINCESS** *(warm, honest)*: So am I, Moss. We can be afraid and brave
at the same time.

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, kind)*: And that is why it is a good thing to
help somebody in the morning, friend. You never know who will help you
in the afternoon.

---

## 11 · ONE BRAVE FLAP AT A TIME

**STORYTELLER** *(gentle, clear)*: Deep inside the cave, the fireflies
stopped. And there was the problem. One big round white stone was stuck
in the mouth of an underground river. The water pushed and pushed behind
the stone, and the water could not get past.

**PRINCESS** *(recognising it)*: That is the stone that fell this
morning. We have to move it. Push!

**STORYTELLER** *(warm)*: Princess pushed the stone as hard as she
could. The stone did not move. Not even a little bit.

**PRINCESS** *(tired, honest)*: It is too heavy for me.

**PIP** *(bright, rallying)*: It is not too heavy for all three of us! I
will pull from the side.

**PRINCESS** *(planning)*: I will push from down here. Moss, we need
somebody on top of the stone.

**MOSS** *(afraid)*: But I cannot fly.

**PRINCESS** *(warm, believing in him)*: You crossed the bridge, Moss.
And you were afraid. Just one brave flap at a time.

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, rising)*: And Moss flapped his wings once. And
he flapped them twice. And up went Moss!

**MOSS** *(amazed, joyful)*: I am flying! Princess, I am really flying!

**PRINCESS** *(strong, leading)*: Ready? One. Two. Three. Push!

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, joyful)*: Princess pushed from below. Pip pulled
from the side. Moss pushed down from the top. And the big white stone
rolled away. And the water came rushing out, bright and shining, out of
the cave and into the forest.

---

## 12 · TWO SILVER BELLS

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, happy)*: Out in the green clearing, Moonwater
Spring filled up with water again. And Moonwater Spring began to shine.
And then Princess heard a sound. It was the sound of a little silver
bell.

*Ring, ring, ring.*

**PRINCESS** *(overjoyed)*: Mama! That is Mama's bell!

**GLITTER** *(calling, full of love)*: Princess!

**STORYTELLER** *(warm)*: And Princess ran to her mum faster than she
had ever run in her life.

**GLITTER** *(tender, holding her)*: Oh, my brave girl. I have been
walking to this spring since the morning.

**PRINCESS** *(happy, understanding)*: You came to our meeting place.

**GLITTER** *(warm, certain)*: Of course I did. That is our rule. I knew
that if I waited here, you would come.

**PRINCESS** *(proud, telling it)*: I stopped, Mama. And I listened. I
could not hear your bell. So I walked to our meeting place. And Pip and
Moss helped me find the way.

**MOSS** *(proud, still amazed)*: And I flew!

**GLITTER** *(warm, moved)*: Then the whole forest will say thank you
tonight. And I have something for you, Princess.

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, tender)*: And Glitter lifted a little silver
bell out from under her mane. It was just the right size for Princess.

**GLITTER** *(tender)*: I am not giving you this bell because you will
never be far from me again. I am giving it to you because now you know
what to do when you are.

**PRINCESS** *(delighted)*: Ring, ring, ring! It sounds just like your
bell, Mama!

---

## 13 · WHAT WE LEARNED TODAY

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, gentle)*: And that was the day the white stones
came down in Rainbow Forest. That was the day Princess could not hear
her mum's bell. And that was the day Princess found her own way to the
meeting place.

**PRINCESS** *(warm, to the audience)*: Friend? I learned something
today. If you cannot find somebody you love, do not run. Stop. Listen.
And go to the place you both know. That is where they will be looking
for you.

**PRINCESS** *(warm, simple)*: And if the way is too big for you, ask a
friend to help. Pip and Moss helped me. We did it together.

**STORYTELLER** *(warm, gentle, closing)*: The evening came down soft
and gold over Rainbow Forest. Moonwater Spring was shining in the
clearing. And on the path home, two silver bells were ringing together.
One big bell, and one little bell.
