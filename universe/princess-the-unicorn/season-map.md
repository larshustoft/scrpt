# Princess the Unicorn — the season map

Three seasons planned from the 26 stories of the original list, re-cut
into blocks that can be produced and released. Every episode standalone;
every season a block. House rules in `universe/SEASON-FORMAT.md`.

## SEASON THREE — "How do I look after something?"
**18 episodes · a whole turning year · the collection: the map of Rainbow
Forest, one new place drawn per episode**

Princess is a little older and becomes the one who teaches. A younger
character — **Pebble**, a foal smaller than she was — arrives in episode
three and is mentored across the season. Themes move outward from the
self: responsibility, promises, fairness, courage on someone else's
behalf, and the forest itself as something to care for. Ends with the
first journey beyond Rainbow Forest, and the map complete.

---

## Why this shape

Twelve is the first season because twelve is producible: twelve books,
twelve audiobooks, twelve films, four compilations, one book box — a
complete, sellable block with a beginning and an end. Eighteen and
twenty-four come later, when the line is fast enough that quality does
not pay for the volume.
