# PRINCESS AND THE HIDDEN SPRING
## Episode 1 — the script (v3, audio-first)

Written to be HEARD. With the eyes closed this is the whole story.
STORYTELLER = David · PRINCESS = Tilly · GLITTER = Beth · PIP = Aurelia
· MOSS = Valf · FIREFLIES = Emily.
Running order: intro (theme) → 1–13 → lullaby ending → TigerWorks card.

---

### 1 · THE MORNING

**STORYTELLER:** Far away, where the rainbows go to rest, there is a
forest. And in that forest, every single morning begins the same sweet
way — with a little silver bell, going ring, ring, ring.

**STORYTELLER:** The bell belongs to Glitter. And the small one skipping
along behind her — that's Princess.

**PRINCESS:** Hi, friend! Come and walk with us. This is my favourite
part of the whole day.

**STORYTELLER:** Every morning they walk the same path, to the same
secret place. But today would not be like every morning. Today, Princess
would help somebody, lose somebody, and find something out about
herself.

**GLITTER:** Stay near my bell, little one. Then you will never get lost.

**PRINCESS:** I love that sound, Mama. Ring, ring, ring — like tiny
music, just for us.

**GLITTER:** *(singing)* Stay near my bell… and you'll never lose your way.

---

### 2 · MOONWATER SPRING

**STORYTELLER:** Their secret place is called Moonwater Spring. The
water there glows soft and silver, like somebody caught the moon and
left it in the grass.

**STORYTELLER:** Glitter dipped her horn and drank. Princess did exactly
the same.

**PRINCESS:** Mmm! It tastes like starlight. And sweet berries!

**GLITTER:** We come here every single morning, my sweet. This water is
what keeps the whole forest growing and shining.

**PRINCESS:** Then I never want to miss it. Not for anything in the
whole wide world.

---

### 3 · THE TINY CRY

**STORYTELLER:** But on the way home, through the morning mist, Princess
heard something very small.

**FIREFLIES:** Help us! Help us, please!

**STORYTELLER:** Under a fallen branch, a family of fireflies was
stuck — their little lights blinking on and off, on and off.

**PRINCESS:** Don't be frightened. I'm coming!

**STORYTELLER:** Princess pushed that branch with her nose. She pushed
and she pushed, and at last it rolled away — and the fireflies went up
into the mist like tiny golden sparks, blinking thank you, thank you.

**PRINCESS:** Helping somebody feels warm. Right here, inside.

---

### 4 · MAMA'S GONE

**STORYTELLER:** And that is how it happened. While Princess was
helping, the fog came rolling in between the trees — and her mother's
bell walked on without her.

**PRINCESS:** Mama? Mama, where are you?

**STORYTELLER:** She listened as hard as she could. But there was no
ring, ring, ring. There was nothing at all.

**PRINCESS:** Stay near the bell, Mama said. But I can't hear the bell.

**STORYTELLER:** Every tree looked like the next tree. Every path looked
like the one before it. And Princess felt something she had never felt
before — a cold, wobbly worry, right in her tummy.

**PRINCESS:** I'm lost. I'm really, really lost.

---

### 5 · PIP

**PIP:** Chirp! Are you lost, little one?

**STORYTELLER:** A small blue bird landed on the branch above her.

**PIP:** I'm Pip! And I know every path in this whole forest.

**PRINCESS:** I lost my mother. I have to find her before it gets dark.

**PIP:** Then we'll find her together. Two heads are better than one!

**STORYTELLER:** And just like that, Princess felt a tiny bit braver.

---

### 6 · THE WOBBLY BRIDGE, AND MOSS

**STORYTELLER:** Pip led her to a wobbly rope bridge, swaying over a
rushing stream. Princess's knees went wobbly just looking at it.

**PRINCESS:** Step by step. I can do this. Step… by… step.

**STORYTELLER:** And she did. All the way across.

**MOSS:** Please don't be scared of me!

**STORYTELLER:** Out of the bushes came a small green dragon, shaking
from his nose to his tail.

**MOSS:** My name is Moss. I'm scared of nearly everything.

**PRINCESS:** Hello, Moss. Have you seen a big unicorn with a rainbow
mane?

**MOSS:** No… but I'll help you look. I'm not very brave. And I can't
fly. But I can walk very, very fast.

**PRINCESS:** Then you're exactly the friend we need.

---

### 7 · THE BOUNCING MUSHROOMS

**STORYTELLER:** So the three new friends walked on together — and the
forest, kind as ever, gave them its bounciest shortcut.

**PRINCESS:** Wheee!

**MOSS:** I'm flying! …Well. Almost flying!

**STORYTELLER:** And for one small moment, Princess almost laughed out
loud. Almost.

---

### 8 · THE RAIN

**STORYTELLER:** Then the clouds rolled over the sun, and the rain came
down in great grey sheets. All the way, Princess had been following her
mother's hoofprints in the soft mud — and now, one by one, the rain
washed them away.

**PRINCESS:** No! Those were our only clues!

**PIP:** Don't worry, Princess. We'll find another way.

**MOSS:** I— I don't like storms.

**PRINCESS:** Stay close to me, Moss. We'll get through it together.

---

### 9 · THE BELL THAT STOPPED

**STORYTELLER:** And far away, through the very same rain, Mama Glitter
was searching too.

**GLITTER:** Princess! Princess, where are you?

**STORYTELLER:** And then — her bell caught fast on a thorny branch. She
pulled, and she pulled. But it would not come free.

**GLITTER:** Oh no. My bell!

**STORYTELLER:** And so the ringing stopped. Now, even if Princess came
close, there would be nothing at all to hear.

---

### 10 · THE SILENT SPRING

**STORYTELLER:** Then Princess had an idea — the kind of idea that comes
from knowing somebody very well.

**PRINCESS:** The spring! Mama goes to Moonwater Spring every single
morning. If we wait there, she'll find us!

**STORYTELLER:** But when they reached it, the spring was not glowing at
all. It sat still, and grey, and quiet.

**PRINCESS:** Where has all the water gone?

**PIP:** Look — over there. It's all running into that dark cave.

**MOSS:** I really, really don't want to go in there.

**PRINCESS:** Neither do I. But if the water can't flow, the whole
forest goes thirsty. Moss — stay right beside me, and you'll be safe.

**MOSS:** Stay beside you. Okay. I can do that.

---

### 11 · THE CRYSTAL IN THE DARK

**STORYTELLER:** Inside, the cave was darker than any night. And then —
softly, silverly — Princess's horn began to glow.

**STORYTELLER:** And there it was: a giant crystal, stuck fast, holding
back the whole underground river.

**PRINCESS:** Push! Push!

**STORYTELLER:** She pushed with all her might. It did not move. Not
even a tiny bit. And Princess began to think that some things are just
too big for someone very small.

**PRINCESS:** Moss… I need your wings.

**MOSS:** I told you. I can't fly.

**PRINCESS:** You don't have to fly far. Just up to the top. I believe
in you.

**STORYTELLER:** Moss flapped once. He flapped twice. And then —

**MOSS:** I'm flying! I'm really flying!

**PRINCESS:** Now — all together! One, two, three… PUSH!

**PIP:** Pulling as fast as I can!

**STORYTELLER:** And the great crystal rolled away with a tremendous
splash, and the water burst free — bright, and glowing, and rushing out
into the forest.

---

### 12 · TWO BELLS

**STORYTELLER:** Moonwater Spring came back to life, shining like the
moon come home. And its light spilled out through the trees — all the
way to a worried mother searching in the rain.

**GLITTER:** *(distant)* Princess! Princess!

**PRINCESS:** Mama!!

**STORYTELLER:** And Princess ran faster than she had ever run in her
whole life, straight into her mother's neck.

**GLITTER:** My brave, clever girl. I have something for you.

**STORYTELLER:** And Glitter gave her a gift: a little silver bell,
just her own size.

**GLITTER:** Now you will always be found. And I will always be able to
hear you.

**PRINCESS:** Ring, ring, ring!

**MOSS:** It sounds just like yours, Glitter. Only smaller!

**PIP:** Two bells are better than one!

---

### 13 · WHAT WE LEARNED TODAY

**STORYTELLER:** And so, that was the day Princess was lost — and found
her own way home.

**PRINCESS:** Friend? I learned something today. When you can't hear
somebody you love… you don't run, and you don't panic. You stop. And
you listen. And you go to the place they always go.

**PRINCESS:** And if you feel small — you ask a friend. Two heads really
are better than one.

**STORYTELLER:** And as the evening came down over Rainbow Forest, two
little bells rang, soft and near. One large. And one small.

---

### THE END — the lullaby

*Fixed ending: Glitter tucks Princess into her moss bed and sings the
short Unicorn Lullaby; Princess falls asleep on the last note.*

> Sleep now… sleep now… the forest holds you near.
> Sleep now… sleep now… your dreams are safe in here.
> Goodnight, goodnight, little unicorn… sleep tight.
