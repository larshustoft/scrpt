# Princess the Unicorn — Season One (26 stories)

The full-universe plan (Lars, 2026-08-30). Every entry is ONE story in
THREE bodies: a picture book (Amazon/KDP), an audiobook (Spotify, and
Audible when opened), and a 12-minute film (YouTube — singles +
compilations). Titles follow the series pattern "Princess and the …"
(one brand, one search shelf on Amazon). Every story obeys the episode
format v2.1: one lesson, one singable STRATEGY LINE, the ladder, learner-
becomes-teacher, bedtime ending. The strategy lines below are the seeds
of 26 songs — an album by the end of the season.

Seasonal spread is deliberate: 1–13 evergreen, 14–18 an autumn→winter→
spring→summer arc for seasonal packaging, 19–25 evergreen, 26 finale.

## The plots

1. **The Hidden Spring** (exists — SC-039): Princess follows a tiny cry
   into the fog, frees the fireflies, gets lost, meets Pip and Moss,
   saves Moonwater Spring, and two bells ring her home.
2. **The Thunder Sky**: the first storm of the year rolls over the
   valley. Princess hides under the moss bed until Glitter teaches her
   the thunder tune; by the third rumble Princess is singing it to
   Moss — who is scared of nearly everything — until the storm becomes
   a drum they count together.
3. **The Broken Wing**: Pip lands crooked and can't fly. Princess wants
   to fix it FAST; Glitter shows her gentle — soft nest, slow words,
   small sips. When the Fireflies' littlest one gets stuck, Princess is
   the gentle one. Pip's first wobbly flight ends the day.
4. **The Waiting Seed**: Princess plants a rainbow seed and wants it
   grown NOW — she sings to it, dances for it, nearly digs it up.
   Glitter shows her the waiting song. All season long the pot sits by
   her moss bed; on the last page, a green tip. (Pays off in #24.)
5. **The Tangled Truth**: Princess accidentally cracks Moss's mushroom
   lantern and hides it in the flowers. The hiding feels heavier every
   scene. Saying it out loud is hard, Moss is sad, they mend the
   lantern together — and it shines brighter through the crack.
6. **The Sharing Tree**: the great berry tree gives its once-a-year
   berries and Princess wants her pile to be the biggest. When Pip's
   pile blows away, sharing turns one sad friend and one guilty
   unicorn into a picnic. The tree drops a bonus branch for sharers.
7. **The New Friend**: a shy river-otter family moves to the far bank.
   The friends stare; the otters stare back. One hello from Princess —
   practiced three ways — opens a bridge-building, mud-sliding
   afternoon. The visitor departs waving; the family stays the cast.
8. **The Grumpy Cloud**: Princess wakes up wrong — everything is too
   loud, too bright, too itchy. A small grey cloud literally follows
   her (our one touch of magic). Naming each feeling shrinks it;
   naming the last one out loud to Glitter pops it into rain that
   grows flowers.
9. **The Little Lost Star**: a young star tumbles into the meadow and
   can't get back up. The friends try bouncing, flying, wishing —
   then kindness: they carry it to the highest hill and the
   Fireflies form a ladder of light. Bedtime under a sky where one
   star winks.
10. **The Try-Again Bridge**: the wobbly bridge from book one, feared
    ever since. Moss wants to visit his cousin across the stream but
    can't cross. Princess — who crossed it once, scared — becomes the
    teacher: three tries, three wobbles, one proud dragon.
11. **The Quiet Day**: the valley is too loud for the littlest
    firefly to sleep. MOSS teaches this one — the scared one knows
    stillness best. Slow steps, soft breath, listening for the
    smallest sounds, until the whole forest plays the quiet game.
12. **The Firefly Night**: Princess's first night walk. Shapes look
    strange until the Fireflies show her: the dark is where their
    light lives. Every shadow gets a friendly name. Ends with the
    lullaby under more stars than any daytime ever showed.
13. **The Rainy Parade**: flower-parade day, and it pours. Princess's
    petals are soaked, her mane is flat, her day is ruined — until
    puddle-stomping invents a better parade. The sun returns for a
    rainbow finish it couldn't have made without the rain.
14. **The Golden Leaf**: Princess's favorite climbing tree turns gold
    and lets its leaves go. She tries to stick them back on. Glitter
    walks her through the forest's changing colors; catching a
    falling leaf grants a wish; the bare tree shows a bird's nest
    you could never see in summer.
15. **The First Snow**: white silence over the valley, and nobody has
    seen it before but Glitter. Wonder, snow-unicorns, cold hooves —
    and warmth as a thing friends make together, breath and blankets
    and a huddle of dragon, bird, foal and fireflies.
16. **The Longest Night**: the year's darkest evening. Glitter's
    tradition: carry a light to every den and nest in the valley.
    One lantern at a time, the dark valley fills with little windows
    of gold. The season's most beautiful film; the holiday evergreen.
17. **The Springtime Door**: the ice on Moonwater Spring cracks open
    like a door; everything wakes at once. A baby bird's first
    flight, mud everywhere, and Princess learning that the winter
    she misses made the spring she loves.
18. **The Summer Song**: the hottest day ever. Princess wants to play
    ALL of it; the valley teaches siesta — shade games, slow floats
    on the stream, the summer hum. Rest as its own adventure.
19. **The Borrowed Bell**: Princess borrows THE bell for a game and
    loses it in the flower field. The search retraces her whole
    joyful careless afternoon. Found at last where she first stopped
    caring for it; polished, returned, and Glitter lets her wear it
    to sleep, once.
20. **The Echo Cave**: the cave by the spring says everything back.
    Grumpy words come home grumpy; the friends discover kind ones
    do too — and that the game works outside the cave, on each
    other, all the way home.
21. **The Upside-Down Day**: everything goes wrong in the silliest
    ways — breakfast in the stream, Pip nesting in Moss's ear, rain
    falling UP (sparkle mischief). Fighting it fails; giggling with
    it wins. The valley's silliest, most quotable episode.
22. **The Smallest Helper**: moving day for the otters and everyone
    big is busy being strong. The littlest firefly feels useless —
    until night falls mid-move and one small spark is the only one
    who can light the way. Small is a kind of mighty.
23. **The Two Paths**: the way to the berry tree forks and Princess
    freezes — what if she chooses wrong? Trying to choose alone
    spins her in circles; asking Pip (who knows every path) is not
    losing, it's how the forest works. Both paths get walked in the
    end.
24. **The Thank-You Garden**: the rainbow seed from #4 has become a
    flower — and Princess never thanked anyone who helped it grow.
    One thank-you at a time (the rain, the sun, Moss's shade-leaf,
    Glitter's waiting song), each thanks planting a new seed, until
    the meadow has a garden the whole valley shares.
25. **The Sleepy Sun**: Princess decides bedtime is unfair and plans
    to stay up FOREVER. Glitter doesn't argue — she walks her
    through the valley's own bedtime: flowers folding, birds
    tucking, the sun rolling under the hills, the fireflies' last
    watch. Being awake alone is lonely; sleep is where the whole
    forest goes together. The brand's thesis episode.
26. **The Best Day Ever**: the friends throw a festival of everything
    — and every game needs something learned this season: sharing
    the feast, waiting for the cake, the try-again race, a thunder
    shower mid-party, one lost little otter found by listening for
    bells. The strategy lines return one by one as a medley; the
    season's last lullaby plays over every friend asleep in one big
    pile. (Film doubles as the season's compilation trailer.)

## The machinery (per story)

Book (SCRPT interior v2 layout → KDP paperback + Kindle) → audiobook
(narration master chain, TigerWorks ident, Lily voice → Spotify via
distribution; Audible when opened) → film (board v2.1 → shoot →
bookends) → YouTube single + rolling compilations. One work order
births all three bodies; the universe profile carries the shared
identity. Production order per story: BOOK first (text + spreads are
the film's source), then audiobook, then film.
