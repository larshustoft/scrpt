# princesstheunicorn.com — The Kingdom Vision (Lars, 2026-08-30)

## The brand thesis (the keystone)
**Princess is the little unicorn who helps children understand big
feelings, learn new things and believe in themselves.**
Not merely a cute unicorn with adventures — a defined role in the
child's life. Room for entertainment, learning, empathy, bedtime and
music; deliberately apart from action/merch-led Unicorn Academy.
Reference brand: Bluey (www.bluey.tv).

## The site IS the kingdom
Opening: Princess beside a glowing path — "Hello! I'm Princess. Would
you like to explore with me?" (her voice). One big button: Enter the
Magical World. Four visual doors: Watch · Listen · Play · Create.
Discreet For Grown-Ups at the bottom. NO nav bars, carousels, long
text, or shop-fronting — a pre-reader must understand the whole thing.

## Five destinations (the growing structure)
| Destination | Content |
|---|---|
| Princess's Castle | Meet Princess, Glitter and friends |
| Story Forest | Episodes, narrated stories, books |
| Rainbow Meadow | Simple games, musical activities |
| Creative Cottage | Coloring pages, drawing, printable crafts |
| Starlight Garden | Lullabies, bedtime stories, calming music |

## Feature roadmap (phased, each honest when it ships)
1. Bedtime Mode (closest — lullaby exists): darker, slower, lullabies.
2. Sing with Princess: karaoke words lighting up (strategy songs!).
3. Princess's Adventure of the Day: tiny two-choice story.
4. Princess's Magic Mirror: pick a feeling → encouragement (THE thesis).
5. Create Your Own Unicorn: colors, mane, wings, horn, power.
6. The Kindness Garden: real-world kind act → flower in the garden.
7. Secret Stars: hidden stars → printable certificates.
8. Birthday Greeting: personalized Princess greeting (parent enters name).

Build principle: evolve the current site (Lars: "pretty good") — hero
first, then kingdom naming, then destinations as content ships.
