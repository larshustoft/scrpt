# The Hidden Spring — storyteller manuscript v2 (for Lars's approval)

The rebalance: narration LEADS the storytelling (12 beats, up from 8,
written as one flowing read-aloud story), dialogue keeps only the lines
that sparkle — the songs, the feelings, the jokes, the lesson. Four
expository dialogue lines convert to narration. Everything is sized to
the existing takes: zero re-shoots, zero credits — re-record + re-cut only.

## What dialogue keeps doing

The songs (Glitter's strategy line and its reprise), Princess's hello
and her handover question to the viewer, every feeling said plainly
("I'm lost." / "Mama!!"), the friendships (Pip's introduction, Moss's
courage), the plan at the spring, the climax teamwork, and the bell
gift. 61 lines fall to ~58 — the cuts are only where a character was
doing the narrator's job.

## The arc the narration now draws

Cosy welcome → who these two are and where they're going → the secret
place and why it matters → the cry for help → how she got lost → hope
(hoofprints) → friendship (the shortcut) → the bell falls silent → the
light calls Mama home → a new sound in the forest → goodnight.
Read alone, eyes closed, it is the whole story.
