# PRINCESS AND THE HIDDEN SPRING
## Episode 1 — SHOOTING MANUSCRIPT v5 (the clear one)

The script is the film. It is written to be FOLLOWED WITH THE EYES CLOSED.
A listener who never sees a picture must know, at every moment: where we
are, what we want, and what just changed.

**Three laws this draft obeys**
1. **One rule, said early.** Glitter gives Princess ONE rule, and it names
   the destination in the first two minutes. The audience knows the plan
   before the trouble starts.
2. **One event causes everything.** A slide of stones on the ridge. It
   traps the fireflies AND it blocks the spring. There are no other
   coincidences in this film.
3. **The rule is what saves her.** Her mother is waiting at the spring
   because that is the rule — not because a light happened to shine.

**Voices** — STORYTELLER: David · PRINCESS: Tilly · GLITTER: Beth
(energized) · PIP: Aurelia · MOSS: Valf · FIREFLIES: Emily
**Running order** — theme intro → scenes 1–13 → lullaby ending → credits.
One voice at a time. The storyteller carries every change of place,
every change of plan, and every cause.

---

## 1 · A MORNING THAT SOUNDS LIKE A BELL

**STORYTELLER:** Far away, where the rainbows go to rest, there is a
place called Rainbow Forest.

*Ring, ring, ring.*

**STORYTELLER:** And every morning in Rainbow Forest begins with that
sound. It belongs to a big unicorn named Glitter, and it hangs on a
ribbon around her neck.

**STORYTELLER:** And the little one, bouncing along behind her — that is
Princess.

**PRINCESS:** Good morning, friend! Mama and I are walking to our
favourite place in the whole forest. Will you come?

**STORYTELLER:** Every single morning, the two of them walk the same
path, to the same place. And this morning began exactly like all the
others. It would not end like them at all.

---

## 2 · THE RULE

**STORYTELLER:** The path went in under the big flowers, where the mist
lies low and soft in the mornings.

**GLITTER:** Stay close enough to hear my bell, little one.

**PRINCESS:** I can always hear it, Mama. It sounds like tiny stars
laughing.

**GLITTER:** And if you ever cannot hear it — do you remember the rule?

**PRINCESS:** Stop. Listen. And go to Moonwater Spring.

**GLITTER:** And I will always, always come there. Every time. That is
our meeting place.

**PRINCESS:** Even if it takes a long, long time?

**GLITTER:** Even then. Stop. Listen. Go to the spring. And wait for me.

**STORYTELLER:** Remember that rule, friend. Princess will need it
before this morning is over.

---

## 3 · MOONWATER SPRING

**STORYTELLER:** Their meeting place had a name: Moonwater Spring. It
sits in a green clearing, at the bottom of a tall white ridge of stones —
and the water in it shines, even in the daytime, like a small piece of
the night sky.

**PRINCESS:** Mmm. It tastes like starlight.

**GLITTER:** With a sprinkle of moonbeam, I think.

**STORYTELLER:** And from that spring, the shining water runs away under
the ground — through the roots, to every flower and every tree and every
creature in Rainbow Forest.

**PRINCESS:** Does the whole forest drink it?

**GLITTER:** Every last drop. That is why we come, every morning, to say
thank you.

**STORYTELLER:** High above them, on top of the white ridge, the stones
sat quiet in the sun. Look at those stones, friend. They matter very
much, in just a moment.

---

## 4 · THE RUMBLE ON THE RIDGE

**STORYTELLER:** They were walking home when the forest made a sound
they had never heard before.

*A long, low rumble.*

**PRINCESS:** Mama? What was that?

**GLITTER:** Just the old stones settling, up on the ridge. Stay close
to me.

**STORYTELLER:** But it was more than that. Far above the clearing, a
whole slope of white stones came sliding down. Dust rose up through the
trees like a thick grey fog. And one round stone rolled straight into
the crack in the rock where the spring comes out — and stopped there,
like a cork in a bottle.

**STORYTELLER:** Nobody saw that happen. Not Glitter. Not Princess. Not
yet.

**STORYTELLER:** And down on the path, the same slide shook a branch
loose — and it came down across the vines with a soft, dry crash.

---

## 5 · THE LITTLE LIGHTS

**FIREFLIES:** Help! Help us, please!

**PRINCESS:** Mama, wait! Somebody is calling!

**STORYTELLER:** Under the fallen branch, a whole family of fireflies
was caught in the vines, their little lights blinking on and off.

**PRINCESS:** Don't be frightened, little lights. I'll get you out.

**STORYTELLER:** So Princess stayed. She lifted the branch with her
horn, carefully, carefully, until every last firefly wriggled free.

**FIREFLIES:** We're free! Thank you, Princess!

**PRINCESS:** Helping somebody makes my heart feel warm.

**STORYTELLER:** And while she was helping — Glitter kept walking. She
was calling Princess's name, and ringing her bell, all the way. But the
dust from the ridge lay thick between them, and the rumble was still
rolling round the trees. Princess never heard a thing.

**PRINCESS:** Mama? I'm finished, Mama — Mama?

**STORYTELLER:** And the path ahead was empty.

---

## 6 · STOP. LISTEN. GO.

**PRINCESS:** Mama!

**STORYTELLER:** Princess started to run.

**PRINCESS:** No. Wait. Running takes me further away.

**STORYTELLER:** So she stopped herself, right there in the dust. And
she remembered the first part of the rule.

**PRINCESS:** Stop. Listen.

**STORYTELLER:** She stood very still, and she listened with her whole
heart.

**PRINCESS:** Can you hear her bell, friend? …No. Neither can I.

**STORYTELLER:** There was no ring, ring, ring anywhere in the forest.
And that is a very big feeling, for somebody very small.

**PRINCESS:** But there's a last part. Stop. Listen. And go to Moonwater
Spring. That's where Mama will be. That's our meeting place.

**STORYTELLER:** And that was Princess's plan, from that moment until
the end of the day: get to Moonwater Spring. She was small, and the fog
was thick, and she was not entirely sure of the way. But she knew where
she was going.

---

## 7 · TWO HEADS

**PIP:** Chirp! Who are you calling for?

**STORYTELLER:** A small blue bird hopped down out of the mist.

**PRINCESS:** My mama. But I know where she'll be — she'll be at
Moonwater Spring. I just don't know the way from here.

**PIP:** Then it's lucky you met me! I'm Pip, and I know nearly every
path in Rainbow Forest.

**PRINCESS:** …Nearly?

**PIP:** The ones that aren't full of dust. But cheer up: two heads are
better than one. Follow me — the spring is that way, past the bridge.

**PRINCESS:** Then let's go. Together?

**PIP:** Together!

**STORYTELLER:** And that is the second thing Princess did right. When
she could not do it alone, she asked.

---

## 8 · THE WOBBLY BRIDGE

**STORYTELLER:** The way to the spring went over a narrow rope bridge,
swaying above a rushing stream.

**PRINCESS:** That bridge looks very wobbly.

**PIP:** It also looks very creaky.

*(the bridge creaks)*

**PIP:** …Extremely creaky.

**MOSS:** Please don't step on my tail!

**STORYTELLER:** Out from under the bridge came a small green dragon,
shaking from his nose to the tip of his tail.

**MOSS:** I'm Moss. I've been under here since the rumble. I want to
cross, but I'm afraid of heights. And deep water. And bridges.
Especially bridges.

**PRINCESS:** We're going to Moonwater Spring to find my mama. Come with
us, Moss. You don't have to be brave all at once.

**MOSS:** How do I do it, then?

**PRINCESS:** One step at a time. Being brave doesn't mean you're not
scared. It means you keep going, even when you are.

**MOSS:** Then… perhaps I can be brave for one bridge.

**STORYTELLER:** Step by step, slow and steady, the three of them
crossed over — and the spring was very close now.

---

## 9 · THE SPRING HAS GONE DARK

**STORYTELLER:** But when they came into the clearing, Princess stopped
so suddenly that Pip bumped into her.

**PRINCESS:** The spring. It's gone dark.

**STORYTELLER:** No shine. No shimmer. No water moving at all — just wet
grey stones, and one thin little trickle.

**PIP:** Where has all the water gone?

**PRINCESS:** Wait. Look up there. The white stones — they've all come
down.

**PRINCESS:** That was the rumble we heard this morning, Mama and me.
The stones came down and they've stopped the water.

**STORYTELLER:** And she was exactly right. The three friends followed
the little trickle, along the rocks, to a crack in the ridge — and
behind the crack, a dark and quiet cave.

**PIP:** In there. That's where the water should come from.

**MOSS:** It is very dark in there.

**PRINCESS:** And if the water doesn't come out, the whole forest goes
thirsty. Mama said every last drop.

**PRINCESS:** But I'm not going in there without a light.

---

## 10 · THE LITTLE LIGHTS COME BACK

**PRINCESS:** Little lights! Are you there? We need you!

**STORYTELLER:** And out of the trees came the fireflies — the very
same family Princess had freed that morning.

**FIREFLIES:** Follow us!

**STORYTELLER:** They flew into the crack in the rock, one after
another, and made a glowing golden trail into the dark.

**PRINCESS:** Moss, stay close to me. Pip, watch the way out behind us.

**MOSS:** I'm still afraid.

**PRINCESS:** So am I. We can be afraid and brave at the same time.

**STORYTELLER:** And that, friend, is why it is worth helping somebody
in the morning. You never know who will light your way in the
afternoon.

---

## 11 · ONE BRAVE FLAP AT A TIME

**STORYTELLER:** Deep inside the cave, the fireflies stopped — and there
it was. One great round white stone, wedged tight in the mouth of the
underground river. The water pushed and pushed behind it, and could not
get through.

**PRINCESS:** That's the one. That's the stone from the ridge.

**PRINCESS:** Push!

**STORYTELLER:** Princess pushed with all her might. It did not move.
Not even a little.

**PRINCESS:** It's too heavy for me.

**PIP:** Not for all three of us! I'll pull from the side.

**PRINCESS:** I'll push from below. Moss — we need somebody up on top.

**MOSS:** But I can't fly.

**PRINCESS:** You crossed a bridge you were afraid of. You don't have to
fly far. Just one brave flap at a time.

**MOSS:** One brave flap at a time…

**STORYTELLER:** And Moss flapped once. And twice. And up he went.

**MOSS:** I'm flying! I'm really flying!

**PRINCESS:** Ready? One — two — three — PUSH!

**STORYTELLER:** Princess pushed from below. Pip pulled from the side.
Moss shoved down from the top. And the great stone rolled away — and the
water came bursting out, bright and shining, rushing past their hooves
and out into the light.

---

## 12 · TWO SILVER BELLS

**STORYTELLER:** Out in the clearing, Moonwater Spring filled up and
began to shine again, like the moon come home.

**STORYTELLER:** And then, from somewhere along the path, came a sound
Princess knew better than any sound in the world.

*Ring, ring, ring.*

**PRINCESS:** Mama!

**GLITTER:** Princess!

**STORYTELLER:** And Princess ran faster than she had ever run in her
life.

**GLITTER:** Oh, my brave girl. I have been walking here since the
morning.

**PRINCESS:** You came to the spring.

**GLITTER:** Of course I did. It is our meeting place. I knew that if I
came here and waited, one day or one hour, you would come too.

**PRINCESS:** I stopped, Mama. And I listened. And when I couldn't hear
you, I came here — and I asked Pip and Moss to help me.

**PIP:** Three heads are better than one!

**MOSS:** And I flew!

**PIP:** …Sort of.

**MOSS:** I definitely flew.

**PRINCESS:** And Mama — the stones came down and stopped the water. We
moved them. The spring is running again.

**GLITTER:** Then the whole forest owes you a thank you tonight. And I
have something for you.

**STORYTELLER:** And from under her mane, Glitter lifted a tiny silver
bell, on a tiny ribbon, just Princess's size.

**GLITTER:** Not because you will never be far from me again. But
because now you know exactly what to do when you are.

**PRINCESS:** Ring, ring, ring! It sounds just like yours!

**MOSS:** Only smaller.

**PIP:** And much more bouncy.

---

## 13 · WHAT WE LEARNED TODAY

**STORYTELLER:** And so that was the day the stones came down in Rainbow
Forest — the day Princess lost her mother's bell, and found her own way
to the meeting place.

**PRINCESS:** Friend? I learned something today. When you can't find
somebody you love, don't run. Stop. Listen. And go to the place you both
know — because that is where they will be looking for you.

**PRINCESS:** And if the way there is too big for you… ask a friend. It
nearly always is, and they nearly always will.

**STORYTELLER:** The evening came down soft and gold over Rainbow
Forest. The spring shone in the clearing. And along the path home, two
silver bells rang together — one big, and one small.

> Sleep now… sleep now… your dreams are safe in here.
> Goodnight, goodnight, little unicorn… sleep tight.
